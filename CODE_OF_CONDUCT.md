# Code of conduct

Symbio is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience, some of
them writing their first pull request. We want taking part to be a good experience for all of them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Help newcomers: explain, point to the docs, and remember everyone started somewhere.
- Keep discussions on topic and in public (issues, pull requests), so others can learn from them.
- Respect people's time: say when you take an issue, and say when you drop it.

## What isn't tolerated

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language, about anyone, for any reason.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, and sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In the project's spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report problems to the maintainer, [@LinKzy](https://github.com/linkzy), privately: a
[private report](https://github.com/linkzy/symbio/security/advisories/new) on GitHub reaches only
the maintainer. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens

The maintainer reviews every report and may, depending on what happened: ask for a change of
behavior, remove or edit comments, code or issues, or block someone from the project, temporarily
or for good.
