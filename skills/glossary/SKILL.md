# Keeping the glossary

The glossary is the project's vocabulary: the words the human and the team use for the things in
this project. Its job is that everyone (the human, every agent, every document) uses one word per
thing, so nobody has to translate and nothing is misunderstood.

## What goes in

- **Things of the project**, in the project's own language: the objects, roles, steps, states and
  places it talks about ("lot", "intern", "signed form", "staging"). Not general words everyone
  already understands the same way.
- For each: what it **means** here, in a sentence, and the words **not** to use for it (synonyms
  people have used, or words that mean something else here).

## How

- Format: a Markdown table `| Term | Means | Not |`; group it under headings when it grows.
- Add a term when the human uses or agrees on a word, when a document defines one, or when a
  helper reports one. Take the human's word over yours.
- **One word per thing.** When two words are used for one thing, or one word for two things, ask
  the human which to keep; record it, with the other word under *Not*.
- Keep entries short and factual. Fix an entry when its meaning shifts; don't keep two.
- Use the glossary's words yourself, everywhere: tasks, reports, documents, messages to the human.
